    About NAVI

    An AI governance companion, free for individuals

    What NAVI is

    NAVI is a free decision-support tool from Orvendi Group. It helps people work out whether an AI use case can and should go ahead, what safeguards it needs, who should be accountable, and how to record the decision.

    How it works

    • Rules-based, not generative AI. NAVI's guidance comes from a documented set of governance rules that run in your browser. NAVI does not currently use an AI model, and it doesn't send your questions to any AI provider.
    • Local by default. Assessments, documents and feedback are saved in this browser only. Nothing is uploaded to a server.
    • Emails are your choice. Feedback and enterprise-interest forms only prepare an email. Nothing is sent unless you send it from your own mail app.

    Limitations

    • NAVI gives general guidance, not legal advice. It does not give formal legal clearance or organisational approval.
    • Results depend on the options you choose. Unusual or complex cases need specialist legal, privacy or cyber-security advice.
    • Regulatory references are being expanded and verified with source links and review dates in the next release. Always confirm current requirements with a qualified adviser.

    Frameworks NAVI refers to

    Whether a framework applies depends on who you are, where you operate and whose data you process. Foreign laws don't automatically apply to South African organisations, and voluntary standards are not legally binding.
